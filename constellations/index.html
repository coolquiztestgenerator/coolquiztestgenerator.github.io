How To Play

Objective: Score the most triangles before the board locks.

Turn Sequence
01 Draw a Line: Tap or drag between two open dots to connect them.
02 Claim & Chain: Closing an empty triangle scores +1 and grants an immediate bonus turn.
03 Pie Rule (Turn 2): Player 2 may swap sides on Turn 2 to claim Player 1's opening line.
✓ Allowed
  • • Sharing endpoints with existing lines
  • • Connecting any two unanchored dots
  • • Chaining multiple bonus turns in a row
✕ Not Allowed
  • • Crossing over any existing line
  • • Passing a line through a 3rd dot
  • • Scoring a triangle with a dot inside it
  • • Connecting to gray locked (anchored) dots
